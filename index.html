About The Story Tree

Nurturing growth through storytelling and training

Inception

This storytelling journey is thoughtfully curated over years and structured in a sequential manner—designed to nurture both learning and life skills. Each story is carefully chosen to spark curiosity, encourage questioning, and foster a love for reading. For children, it also becomes a joyful way to enhance public speaking skills, build confidence, and gently let go of inhibitions; it is a special space where all my programs come together to create a lasting experience for young children.

Vision

The Story Tree is created as a direct result of passion for nurturing via training individuals from across social strata's and defying age as a barrier for learning. At The Story Tree, we believe that life is a journey full of learning experiences and aimed at self and community growth!

About the Founder

Meet Pallavi Singh - Storyteller, Trainer & Educator

Pallavi is a storyteller, trainer, and coach who brings together the worlds of education, hospitality, and human connection with rare ease. She holds an MBA from the Symbiosis Institute of Management Studies, Pune, and a B.Ed. from IGNOU.

Her professional journey: From an early career in banking, she transitioned into heritage hospitality as the Managing Director of Naila Bagh Palace while also serving as a Director in a private hospitality company. Alongside this, she was also an Executive Board Member of a school management committee for some years.

Over the years, one thread quietly tied all her roles together—Training and coaching.

Today, Pallavi draws deeply from her learning and lived experiences to work with children and adults alike, using stories as a powerful tool to build imagination, confidence, clarity, and communication skills. She has conducted corporate trainings with organisations such as Genpact and Citibank (during her tenure as an employee) and has led numerous workshops and interactive sessions for children across premier schools, cultural hubs, and activity centres in Jaipur.

Through her work, she focuses on nurturing creativity, strengthening interpersonal skills, and making communication feel natural and joyful—often turning learning into an experience rather than a lesson.

At The Story Tree, Pallavi brings a sensitive, intuitive approach to helping individuals find their voice—one story, one smile, and many aha! moments at a time.
